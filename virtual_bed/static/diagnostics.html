What the CNN receives

  • The complete 32 × 64 mat grid, exactly as stored. Nothing is cropped, re-centred or padded, and the mat’s position on the bed is not an input.
  • Values are 0.1 mmHg counts. They are clipped at 1,000 counts (100 mmHg), then log-scaled: log(1 + v) ÷ log(1001). The training recordings were raw counts on a 0–1,000 scale; treating one count as 0.1 mmHg is an assumption, not a calibration.
  • Your posture setting, the body position and the coverage figure are stored with the frame for comparison only. The API never passes them to the model.
  • A frame where every sensor reads zero is stored as “no load” and the model is not run. The CNN only learned occupied-bed postures.
  • Load outside the sensing area is not measured. A body partly off the mat reaches the CNN as a truncated pressure map.

Model card

Modelpmat-flat-v1, PostureCNN (2 convolution layers, 3 classes)
Trained onPhysioNet PmatData Experiment I, flat-bed positions, subjects 1–8; validation subjects 9–10
Held-out testSubjects 11–13: accuracy 98.9%, macro F1 0.989 (recorded frames, not simulated)
OutputsSoftmax scores for back, right side, left side. Scores are not calibrated probabilities.
Not supportedEmpty beds, a turned or partly covered body, other mats or mattresses, sitting, and anything clinical. It does not assess pressure-injury risk.
